# ⏰ TEMPORAL EXPLORATION - QUICK REFERENCE

## What Was Done
Analyzed 419,826 Flickr photos across 28.8 years to understand **when people photograph Lyon**.

---

## 🎯 Top 5 Discoveries

### 1. **December is King** 📊
```
December: 61,748 photos (14.7% of ALL data)
February: 21,304 photos (5.1% of all data)
Ratio: 2.9x more photos in peak month vs slowest month
```
**Why:** Holiday tourism + Christmas break travel

### 2. **2009 Changed Everything** 📱
```
2008: 992 photos
2009: 14,282 photos  ← iPhone 3GS (GPS + camera)
Growth: +1,340% in one year
```
**Why:** Smartphone photography revolution

### 3. **Summer is Second Peak** ☀️
```
July: 44,910 photos (10.7%)
Summer season: 112,788 total (26.9%)
School break + vacation travel
```

### 4. **Growth Stopped in 2015** 📈
```
2010-2014: Rapid growth (40k→45k photos/year)
2015-2019: Plateau at ~40k-41k/year (no growth)
Why: Market saturation + Flickr decline + Instagram rise
```

### 5. **Surprisingly Balanced Year** 🌡️
```
Winter : 25.8%
Spring : 25.4%
Summer : 26.9%  ← Only 1.5% more than winter!
Autumn : 22.0%

Conclusion: Lyon is year-round destination
```

## 🧠 What This Means

✅ **Lyon is a year-round destination**
- Balanced across all seasons (only 5% variance)
- Suitable indoor/outdoor attractions
- Consistent domestic + international tourism

✅ **Strong holiday effect**
- December = 14.7% of annual volume
- 2.9x higher than slowest month
- Global travel patterns reflected in photos

✅ **Technology adoption visible**
- Clear inflection in 2009 (smartphone era begins)
- Plateau in 2015 (market maturity)
- Demonstrates power of GPS + camera combo

✅ **Summer vacation peak**
- July = 2nd most active month
- School break travel patterns
- Warm weather tourism
