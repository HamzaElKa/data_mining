# TEMPORAL DATA EXPLORATION - EXECUTIVE REPORT

## What Was Explored

A comprehensive **temporal analysis** of 419,826 geotagged Flickr photos from Lyon, France, spanning nearly 29 years (February 1991 - November 2019).

---

## 🎯 Main Findings

### 1️⃣ **Peak Activity: December (14.7% of all photos)**
- 61,748 photos concentrated in December (Christmas holidays)
- 2.9x more photos than February (the quietest month)
- Reflects global holiday tourism patterns

### 2️⃣ **Peak Year: 2014 (45,051 photos)**
- Years 2014-2018 represent the "golden period"
- ~43,000-45,000 photos per year during this period
- Represents the mature phase of smartphone photography adoption

### 3️⃣ **Growth Inflection: 2009**
- Explosive growth from 992 (2008) → 14,282 (2009) = +1,340%
- Coincides with iPhone 3GS release (GPS + camera)
- Marks transition from digital to smartphone photography era

### 4️⃣ **Seasonal Balance**
- Summer: 26.9% of photos
- Winter: 25.8% of photos  
- Spring: 25.4% of photos
- Autumn: 22.0% of photos
- **Insight:** Only 4.9% variation = year-round destination

### 5️⃣ **Plateau Effect (2015+)**
- Stabilized at ~40k photos/year after 2015
- No further growth despite maturity
- Likely due to Flickr decline + Instagram rise

## 💡 Insights & Interpretation

### Why December Peaks
- Winter holidays (Dec 20-Jan 5)
- Christmas break tourism  
- Festive decorations attract photographers
- New Year's travel

### Why February is Slowest
- Shortest month
- Coldest weather (discourages tourism)
- Post-holiday slump (no major events)
- Fewest vacation days in academic calendar

### Why 2009 Was Watershed
- iPhone 3GS: First iPhone with GPS
- Location-based social networks emerging
- Flickr mobile apps became viable
- "Photo sharing" became mainstream

### Why Growth Plateaued After 2015
- Market saturation (everyone has GPS camera)
- Flickr's peak popularity reached
- Instagram emerging as primary photo platform
- "Check-in culture" shifted to other apps
